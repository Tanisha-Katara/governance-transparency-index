Methodology

What is this index?

The Governance Transparency & Engagement Index is a composite score measuring how open, communicative, and active Filecoin’s governance processes are each month. It combines four categories into a single weighted score between 0% and 100%.

Categories & Weights

  • Published Governance Artifacts (30%) — Availability of governance docs, meeting notes, and decision records. Max raw score: 10 points.
  • Core Devs Transparency (35%) — Openness of core developer processes, meeting accessibility, and decision visibility. Max raw score: 6 points.
  • Governance Communications (25%) — Quality and frequency of governance updates and community announcements. Max raw score: 9 points.
  • GitHub Activity Index (10%) — FIP/FRC proposal activity, PR throughput, and community engagement. Max raw score: 18 points.

Scoring

Each category is scored as a ratio: actual points / max points, yielding a value between 0 and 1. The final index is the weighted sum:

Index = 0.30 × Artifacts + 0.35 × CoreDevs + 0.25 × Comms + 0.10 × GitHub

Quarterly Aggregation

Quarters are the simple average of their constituent monthly index scores.

How to Update

Open generate_governance_index.py, add a new entry to the MONTHLY_SCORES dictionary, then run python3 generate_governance_index.py. The updated HTML will be written to governance-index.html.
